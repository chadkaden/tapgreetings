How TapGreetings NFC Smart Cards Work

A seamless blend of physical polymer clay keepsake trading cards and instant 1080p stop-motion claymation video streaming.

1

Hold Your Phone Near the Card Antenna

Ensure your smartphone screen is unlocked. On iPhone (iPhone XS, XR, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16), hold the very top edge of your phone against the card's golden motif. On Android, hold the center back of your device against the card.

2

Tap the Browser Notification Banner

A native notification banner from your phone's browser will appear instantly (e.g. "Open TapGreetings.com in Safari / Chrome"). Zero app downloads are required—NFC works directly via your phone's native operating system.

3

Enjoy 1080p Video, Sound & 3D Keepsake Lore

The card instantly launches the custom stop-motion animation video and holiday voice blessing. Tap unmuting anytime to listen, or flip to inspect the high-resolution artwork and collectible stats.

🛡️ Card Hardware & Microchip Specifications

Physical Dimensions: CR80 standard credit card size (85.60 mm × 53.98 mm × 0.76 mm / 30 mil).
Durability: 100% waterproof solid PVC core with protective scratch-resistant matte overlay and gold-foil stamping.
NFC Microchip: NXP NTAG215 (504 bytes user memory, 13.56 MHz, ISO/IEC 14443 Type A compliant).
Reprogrammability: Fully unlocked & rewritable. Reprogram anytime with free NFC tools or our built-in web portal.
Compatibility: iPhone XS through iPhone 16 Pro, and all NFC-enabled Android devices.
