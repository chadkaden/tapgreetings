How TapGreetings NFC Smart Cards Work

A revolutionary blend of tangible artisan keepsake cards and instant digital stop-motion video streaming.

1

Hold Your Phone Near the Card

Ensure your smartphone screen is unlocked. On iPhone (XS or newer), hold the very top edge of your phone against the card's golden antenna icon. On Android, hold the center back of your device against the card.

2

Tap the Notification Banner

A native notification banner from your phone's browser will appear instantly (e.g. "Open TapGreetings.com in Safari / Chrome"). No special third-party app download is ever required!

3

Enjoy 1080p Video & Voice Dialogue

Watch your physical character come alive in cinematic 1080p vertical video, listen to festive audio dialogue, view behind-the-scenes artisan prompts, and explore character stats.
