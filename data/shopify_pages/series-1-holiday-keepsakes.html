How TapGreetings NFC Works

Every card is embedded with a high-speed NTAG215 microchip built into waterproof CR80 PVC hardware.

1

Hold Phone Near Antenna

Hold top edge of iPhone or center back of Android near the golden bell, star, or ribbon icon on the card.

2

Tap Screen Notification

A native browser banner appears instantly. Tap to open the high-definition video card with zero app downloads.

3

Stream Claymation & Voice

Enjoy full 1080p stop-motion animation, voice blessings, and tactile 3D keepsake details on your phone!
