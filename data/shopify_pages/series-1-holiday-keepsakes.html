How TapGreetings Smart Cards Work

Each physical card contains an embedded high-speed NTAG215 microchip. No batteries, no charging, no mobile apps required!

1️⃣ 🎁
Give the Physical Keepsake
Deliver the credit-card-sized, waterproof polymer clay card in person or in a greeting envelope.
2️⃣ 📱
Tap with Smartphone
Touch the top edge of any iPhone or the back of any Android phone to the card's golden NFC target.
3️⃣ 🎬
Experience the Magic
The browser instantly opens to full 1080p stop-motion claymation, holiday dialogue, and character lore!
